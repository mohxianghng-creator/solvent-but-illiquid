# V4 analysis protocol — fixed before the new economic/control experiments

14 October 2026. Within-session analysis plan, not external preregistration.

Scope: a normalised fixed-income liquidity pool and incurred-claims runoff; NOT full-company valuation or a calibrated Tower replica. Primary case is no assumed bank facility: a public annual report does not establish a committed executable credit line. Optional facilities are explicitly hypothetical stress/alternative cases.

1. Economic objective: minimise expected accumulated cash opportunity cost plus actual execution expense and accrued funding cost, subject to TRAINING payment reliability. No shortfall/capital penalty is inserted into monetary cost. If no cash-policy candidate passes a simultaneous exact upper-bound screen, report infeasible; do not silently pick an unsafe minimum.
2. All cash endpoints remain permitted. An interior answer is not required or forced. Positive yield spread makes unnecessary cash costly; market/SCR diagnostics are kept separate from the liquidity objective. Yield spreads 0.5/1/2 percentage points are sensitivities, not confidence estimates.
3. Daily business-day settlement and cash demands. Base settlement lag 2 business days; test 0/1/5. Actual source settlement scope must be stated. Daily cash allocation within public reporting intervals is synthetic, NOT observed deadlines.
4. Cash grid [0,.25,.5,1,2,4,8,12,20,40,80,maximum], plus source-scaled disclosed accessible cash benchmark. Screen grid-policy combinations with simultaneous one-sided Clopper-Pearson training bounds. Training n=1000; test n=3000; separate rule tuning n=250. Seeds 202610400/401/402. Test must not select policy or cash.
5. Main strong simple comparator: choose sale-order and forecast-buffer horizon/quantile on tuning paths, same available observations and source constraints as MPC. Also report a fixed myopic rule as diagnostic. All policies share settlement lag, holdings, trading costs, capacities, credit and claims priority.
6. Forecast: latent ultimate losses not observed by controllers. Synthetic noisy log reports update a normal posterior; cumulative known claim demands are a lower bound. Controllers receive immutable observed state + forecast vector only, never hidden future arrays. Forecast distribution is not empirically calibrated; simulation coverage diagnostics required.
7. Forward controller: finite-horizon linear receding-horizon programme with cash, inventory, unsettled sales and borrowing. First minimise forecast unmet payment exposure lexicographically; then minimise the SAME cash/transaction/financing cost components reported externally. Full-horizon/terminal funding charges disclosed. No claim of a nonlinear global optimum.
8. Public data: preserve nominal paid amounts, lower bounds and closed-claim counts separately. Fit grouped events without pooling related NZ2023 snapshots across train/test groups. New Kaikoura and Australian flood evidence is held out for external transfer checks. Estimate revisions and incomplete ultimate denominators remain explicit. Do not infer company-level weekly due dates.
9. Company data: official Tower annual report, two comparative years; reconcile cash, investments, maturity buckets, restricted cash and operating/investing/financing cash. Undisclosed facilities are NOT inferred. Coupon/portfolio yield proxies must not be called marginal expected bond returns.
10. Comparison: fixed common cash 2 and separately training-selected cash, paired failure differences with exact discordance tests and Holm, paired cost bootstrap. If risk reliability is not matched, costs must not be called an efficiency improvement. No safety/equivalence inference from degenerate bootstrap intervals.
11. Robustness: held-out timing families, yield spread, source cash restrictions, forecasting noise/bias, sale capacity, settlement lag, facility access and 52-week continuation. Results exploratory outside primary contrasts. Non-interior and negative results retained.
12. Release: independent ledger and economic cost oracle; controller prefix-invariance; no pending-sale reuse; restricted-cash enforcement; partial-information posterior tests; same-objective exact small-horizon benchmark; generated reports and documented defect corrections. Independent professional review remains outstanding.


Pre-final-run software assurance amendment: initial tuning exposed omission of stochastic DAILY payment-allocation uncertainty from the forecast envelope. A report-only loss quantile is not a payment quantile. Before assessing the training/test results, replace it with conditional Dirichlet/Beta cumulative timing envelopes AND the loss-report quantile, applied identically to both rules and MPC. Their combination is not advertised as an exact joint quantile. The simple rule includes a conservative coupon-income allowance. Use fresh tuning/training/test seeds 202610410/411/412 for the final run. The initial tuning exercise is development, not confirmatory evidence. All policy/cash choices remain training/tuning only.

Release reconciliation (transparent scope correction): the final comparative tuning uses nine horizon/quantile combinations PER family, with a fixed liquid-first sale order for both. Sale-order tuning in item 5 was not performed. Fixed myopic and fixed five-day/q90 rules are separately reported as descriptive diagnostics, not additional confirmatory contrasts. The primary yield spread is the source-derived 1.0759pp proxy; 0.5/2pp are main sensitivities, with zero/negative spread covered by no-claims economic endpoint checks. Finite-lookahead MPC is not advertised as solving the outer reliability constraint optimally. A short-horizon analytic first action accelerates only a guarded special case and is checked against the original two-phase LP on 120 random states.

Settlement-horizon amendment: the five-day settlement sensitivity exposed horizon blindness when a three-day MPC could not see sale proceeds arrive. Both forward families now use effective horizon max(tuned horizon, lag+1), truncated at the experiment end. The deliberately myopic diagnostic is exempt. The primary lag2 / locked horizons3 and5 are unchanged; the affected lag5 sensitivity is recomputed and the original development rows retained in work. This correction is disclosed rather than silently treating a known execution lag as unpredictable risk.
